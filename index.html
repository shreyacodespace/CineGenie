✨ Meet The Founder

Shreya (Alice)

Dreamer • Creator • Builder

CineGenie was inspired by my fascination with visual storytelling, cinematography, and the creative process behind filmmaking.

During the COVID years, I began experimenting with video creation alongside my family. What started as a simple hobby gradually became a deeper interest in editing, storytelling, cinematic visuals, camera techniques, and creative direction.

While exploring countless short films, breakdown videos, and visual storytelling techniques, I realized how challenging filmmaking can be for beginners. CineGenie was imagined as a creative AI companion that helps storytellers transform ideas into cinematic experiences.

This project combines my curiosity for technology with my admiration for filmmaking, proving that creativity and innovation can work together to empower creators.
