✨ Meet The Founder

Shreya aka Alice

Dreamer • Creator • Explorer

Passionate about storytelling, visual creativity, and the art of filmmaking. Bringing cinematic visions to life through technology and imagination.

During the COVID years, while many people were creating content and following online trends, I started making videos with my mother and sister just for fun. At that time, I knew almost nothing about professional editing software. Curious to improve my videos, I spent hours searching, learning, and experimenting until I discovered a simple free editing app. What began as a small experiment slowly grew into a lasting creative hobby.

As I kept researching, editing, and experimenting with videos, I found myself diving deeper into the world of filmmaking. Watching countless short films, cinematic sequences, and visual storytelling breakdowns sparked something unexpected in me. I became fascinated by cinematography—the way lighting, colors, camera movement, framing, and emotion can transform simple ideas into unforgettable experiences.

That growing curiosity eventually inspired the idea behind CineGenie: a platform imagined for creators who want guidance, inspiration, and cinematic direction while bringing their stories to life. It represents my admiration for the creative process and the dream of making filmmaking tools more accessible for everyone.

The thought that a person can imagine a visual world so beautifully and then bring it to life on screen continues to inspire me. Every masterpiece begins with an idea, and every great story deserves a cinematic vision.

I don't know if life will ever give me the opportunity to fully pursue cinematography and filmmaking as an art, but if that chance ever comes, I would embrace it wholeheartedly and explore it with everything I have.

Life is like series of rat races. Like many others, I find myself moving from one rat race to another. It began with JEE, spending 3 years immersed in Physics, Chemistry and Maths. One chapter has come to an end and another has begun with programming and technology. But beyond careers, exams and achievements, I believe its important to keep searching for the passions that truly move us. Sometimes a single unexpected interest can bring more meaning, joy and most important "peace" within us than any destination we planned for.🐭
